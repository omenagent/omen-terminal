          Technical FAQ

          Where does the data come from?

          Market data from api.dexscreener.com and security flags from api.gopluslabs.io, both called directly from your browser. There is no OMEN backend in between and no demo data.

          How is the score calculated?

          Start at 100. Honeypot → 0. Contract: not open source −25, mintable −20, proxy −15, tax >5% −15 or >15% −35. Owner powers: cannot sell all −40, owner can change balances −35, creator made honeypots before −30, hidden owner / reclaimable ownership / self-destruct −25 each, buying restricted or tax changeable −20, pausable transfers / blacklist / per-wallet tax −15, cooldown −10, external calls −5. Soft-rug: one wallet controls >50% of unlocked LP −25, otherwise <90% of LP locked or burned −10. Origin: impersonates an official Stock Token or stablecoin −40, flagged as scam on the explorer −40, token younger than 24h −20 or 7 days −10, deployer launched 10+ contracts −20 or 5+ −10, deployer holds >25% −20 or >10% −10. Market: liquidity <$50k −20 or <$10k −35, top-10 wallets >50% −20. Official Robinhood Stock Tokens are scored on DEX liquidity only, because pausing, upgrades and compliance blacklists are expected for a regulated issuer. ≥75 Safe to Allocate, 45–74 Caution, <45 Critical.

          How does OMEN know a Stock Token is official?

          Every official Robinhood Stock Token is deployed by one contract, the StockFactory at 0x4783…C046. OMEN asks the Robinhood Chain explorer who created the token. If it is the StockFactory, the token gets the Official badge. If the ticker or name copies a stock but the creator is anyone else, it is flagged as a look-alike.

          Can I share a scan?

          Yes. The address bar updates to #0x… after every scan, so the link opens the same token for anyone. Use Copy link, Share on X or Telegram above the dashboard.

          What is a soft-rug and how does OMEN catch it?

          A token with clean code whose liquidity sits in one wallet. When buyers pile in, that wallet pulls the pool and holders are left with nothing to sell into. OMEN reads who owns the LP positions and how much is locked or burned, and deducts when a single unlocked owner controls most of it.

          Which pair is used when a token trades in many pools?

          The pair with the highest liquidity.usd. Thin pools are easy to manipulate, so the deepest pool is the most honest price.

          Which chain does OMEN support?

          Only Robinhood Chain, the Ethereum L2 by Robinhood (chain ID 4663, ETH for gas). Tokens on other chains are rejected. Verify any address on robinhoodchain.blockscout.com.

          Why scan by address instead of ticker?

          Anyone can deploy a token with the same name. A copycat can show more liquidity than the original, and ticker search picks the deepest pool, so it can land on the fake. When several tokens share a ticker the Agent warns you; always confirm the contract address from the project's official links.

          How does OMEN treat stablecoins?

          Official stablecoins are recognised by exact contract address, never by ticker. On Robinhood Chain that is USDG (Global Dollar, issued by Paxos) at 0x5fc5…d168. Its upgradeable proxy, pause and compliance controls are issuer controls, so they are not scored as rug signals. Any other token that uses a stablecoin ticker or name is flagged as a fake (−40).

          Can GoPlus be wrong on Robinhood Chain?

          Yes. Tokens from the Uniswap UERC20 v2.0.0 factory are wrongly flagged as honeypots with a hidden owner. OMEN recognises that factory, marks those flags as disputed, and only clears the honeypot flag when the token has real sells in the last 24 hours. Some fields can also come back empty and show N/A; treat the score as a first filter and check the explorer.

          Does top-10 concentration include LP and burn wallets?

          No. DEX pools (including the Uniswap v4 PoolManager) and burn addresses are removed first, and their shares are shown separately, so the bar measures real wallets only.

          Does Sign In take custody of anything?

          No. It asks your wallet for its public address only. OMEN never requests a seed phrase, private key or transaction signature.
